Ukraine · Zaporizhzhia

ZAPORIZHZHIA HIT OVERNIGHT

Russian forces struck an apartment block in Zaporizhzhia overnight, killing 2 people and wounding 40, among them 4 children, regional governor Mykhailo Semikin said.

Russia · Ukraine

PUTIN VOWS REVENGE FOR REFINERY STRIKES

Putin told Kremlin officials Kyiv ignored Trump’s direct plea to stop hitting refineries and vowed retaliation; Ukrainian drone strikes have cut Russian refining output 45% since late October. The EU will halt Russian LNG imports from 1 January.

Ukraine · Libya

KYIV CALLS SHIP ATTACKS SELF-DEFENCE

Foreign minister Sybiha insisted the weekend attack on Russian ships in Libyan waters was lawful self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter; Zelenskyy confirmed Ukraine also hit the cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, saying it carried weapons.

South Korea

RUSSIAN JETS SPARK ALERT OFF ULLEUNG

Several Russian aircraft entered South Korea’s air defence identification zone near Ulleung island; fighters scrambled. An emergency alert that first blamed a suspected North Korean plane was corrected about 40 minutes later: Seoul’s military said the jets did not violate Korean airspace. The fourth such entry this year.

Iran · Washington

US WEIGHS TARGETING IRAN’S LEADERS

Vice-President Vance and Secretary Rubio met Qatar’s prime minister, Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani, separately at the White House on Monday. NBC reports Trump officials are discussing targeting senior Iranian leaders to press Tehran to end the war; critics warn it could harden the regime and wreck talks.
